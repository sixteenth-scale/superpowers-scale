RULE 01

Nothing above the skull counts

Batman's 6′2″ is his height as a man. The cowl ears rise proud of that line. Model the body to the target, then add the topper above it — compressing the figure so the ears fit under the target is how a custom ends up visibly short next to a real Kenner figure.

RULE 02

Back off a millimeter for big hair

A listed height is effectively measured to the top of the skull. When a sculpt carries volume above that — Joker's pompadour, Barda's mane — subtract about a millimeter so the silhouette reads right instead of running tall. At 1:16, one millimeter is sixteen real inches, so keep the correction small.
